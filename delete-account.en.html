Delete your account and data

“Path of Islamic Knowledge” game

You can delete your account and all its data yourself at any time, from the app or from a browser.

Steps

  1. Open the game in the app, or in a browser at engebra87.github.io/darb.
  2. Sign in to your account if you aren't signed in.
  3. In the main menu, under your name, tap “Delete account”.
  4. Tap “Delete my account permanently” to confirm.

What gets deleted

  • Your email address and password.
  • The name shown on the leaderboard.
  • Your points, level and game progress.
  • Your house and country in the “Land of the Seekers of Knowledge”.

All of this is deleted immediately and permanently. We keep no copy.

If you use the same account in another of our apps, all game data is deleted and the account stays for that app.

Can't sign in to your account?

Write to us on the feedback page with “Account deletion request” and the name you use on the leaderboard, without posting your email publicly. We'll contact you to complete the deletion within 30 days.
