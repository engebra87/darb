Path of Islamic Knowledge

A 3D game where you walk through the old alleys of Damascus. Each door opens with a correct answer, and the stone wall at the end of each alley falls with the last answer. You collect gold coins, jump over barrels and stones, and greet the shopkeepers.

Levels

  • Level one · Alleys of Damascus: two-tone stone, mashrabiya windows and covered passages.
  • Level two · Marble alleys: marble decorated with Arabic inlay.
  • Level three · Citadel of Damascus: between its walls up to the great citadel.
  • Level four · Markets of Damascus: wide markets with guards at the doors, and questions from “Riyad as-Salihin”.

Each level starts with a first alley, then from the courtyard you choose between the gates of Fiqh, the Prophet's Life (Seerah) and Hadith. The questions get harder as you go.

Land of the Seekers of Knowledge

For every alley you finish, workers build a part of your Damascene house in a large 3D garden, until it is complete with 12 parts. Houses are grouped into quarters by country, and you can visit a friend's house.

Languages

Arabic, English, French, Spanish, Indonesian, Hindi and Urdu. The game picks your device's language automatically, and you can change it with the 🌐 button in the main menu.

Questions

More than 200 questions, written from the Holy Quran, the authentic Sunnah, the Prophet's biography and Imam al-Nawawi's “Riyad as-Salihin”. If you find a question with a mistake, or with more than one possible answer, please tell us so we can fix it.

Report a mistake in a question

Controls

  • Drag your finger anywhere on the screen to walk.
  • The jump button in the corner jumps over obstacles.
  • The ❚❚ button at the top pauses the game.
