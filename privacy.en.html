Privacy Policy

“Path of Islamic Knowledge” game · Last updated: October 31, 2026

We collect as little data as possible, and we use it only to run the game.

Playing without an account

If you play without an account, nothing about you reaches us. Your progress (points, level, finished gates, sound setting) is saved only in your browser on your device, and is erased if you clear your browser data.

If you create an account

We store only the following on Supabase servers:

  • Your email address, to sign you in and send the confirmation code or password reset link.
  • Your password, encrypted so that no one can read it.
  • The name you choose for the leaderboard.
  • Your game progress: points, level and finished gates.
  • The country you choose for your house in the “Land of the Seekers of Knowledge”, and how many parts you have built.

Other players see on the leaderboard only your chosen name, points and level. Your email is never shown to anyone.

Land of the Seekers of Knowledge

In the “Land of the Seekers of Knowledge”, players see your house in your country's quarter: only your chosen name, your house and how many parts it has. A signed-in player who knows your full email can search for it to find your house; they see the name, the house and the country, never the email. If you don't want this, turn off “People who know my email can find my house” in the garden.

What we don't do

  • No ads, and no tracking or analytics tools.
  • We don't sell your data or share it with anyone.
  • We don't ask for your location, camera, microphone or contacts.

Services the game relies on

  • GitHub Pages: hosting the game's pages.
  • Supabase: accounts, questions and progress.
  • Google Fonts, cdnjs and jsDelivr: loading fonts and the libraries that run the game.

Like any website, these services may automatically log your device's IP address when a page loads, and each has its own privacy policy.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account and all its data yourself at any time: in the main menu, tap “Delete account” and confirm. Your email, name, progress and house are deleted immediately and permanently. Details on the account deletion page.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. For young players, we recommend playing without an account, or creating the account with a parent's supervision.
