Privacy Policy

“Path of Islamic Knowledge” game · Last updated: October 31, 2026

We collect as little data as possible, and we use it only to run the game.

Playing without an account

If you play without an account, nothing about you reaches us. Your progress (points, level, finished gates, sound setting) is saved only in your browser on your device, and is erased if you clear your browser data.

If you create an account

We store only the following on Supabase servers:

  • Your email address, to sign you in and send the confirmation code or password reset link.
  • Your password, encrypted so that no one can read it.
  • The name you choose for the leaderboard.
  • Your game progress: points, level and finished gates.
  • The country you choose for your house in the “Land of the Seekers of Knowledge”, and how many parts you have built.

Other players see on the leaderboard only your chosen name, points and level. Your email is never shown to anyone.

Land of the Seekers of Knowledge

In the “Land of the Seekers of Knowledge”, players see your house in your country's quarter: only your chosen name, your house and how many parts it has. A signed-in player who knows your full email can search for it to find your house; they see the name, the house and the country, never the email. If you don't want this, turn off “People who know my email can find my house” in the garden.

What we don't do

  • No ads, and no tracking or analytics tools.
  • We don't sell your data or share it with anyone.
  • We don't ask for your location, camera, microphone or contacts.

Services the game relies on

  • GitHub Pages: hosting the game's pages.
  • Supabase: accounts, questions and progress.
  • Google Fonts, cdnjs and jsDelivr: loading fonts and the libraries that run the game.

Like any website, these services may automatically log your device's IP address when a page loads, and each has its own privacy policy.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account and all its data yourself at any time: in the main menu, tap “Delete account” and confirm. Your email, name, progress and house are deleted immediately and permanently. Details on the account deletion page.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. For young players, we recommend playing without an account, or creating the account with a parent's supervision.

Path of Islamic Knowledge

A 3D game where you walk through the old alleys of Damascus. Each door opens with a correct answer, and the stone wall at the end of each alley falls with the last answer. You collect gold coins, jump over barrels and stones, and greet the shopkeepers.

Levels

  • Level one · Alleys of Damascus: two-tone stone, mashrabiya windows and covered passages.
  • Level two · Marble alleys: marble decorated with Arabic inlay.
  • Level three · Citadel of Damascus: between its walls up to the great citadel.
  • Level four · Markets of Damascus: wide markets with guards at the doors, and questions from “Riyad as-Salihin”.

Each level starts with a first alley, then from the courtyard you choose between the gates of Fiqh, the Prophet's Life (Seerah) and Hadith. The questions get harder as you go.

Land of the Seekers of Knowledge

For every alley you finish, workers build a part of your Damascene house in a large 3D garden, until it is complete with 12 parts. Houses are grouped into quarters by country, and you can visit a friend's house.

Languages

Arabic, English, French, Spanish, Indonesian, Hindi and Urdu. The game picks your device's language automatically, and you can change it with the 🌐 button in the main menu.

Questions

More than 200 questions, written from the Holy Quran, the authentic Sunnah, the Prophet's biography and Imam al-Nawawi's “Riyad as-Salihin”. If you find a question with a mistake, or with more than one possible answer, please tell us so we can fix it.

Report a mistake in a question

Controls

  • Drag your finger anywhere on the screen to walk.
  • The jump button in the corner jumps over obstacles.
  • The ❚❚ button at the top pauses the game.

Thanks

Made with three.js, and the IBM Plex Sans Arabic and Reem Kufi fonts (OFL license). The “Ahsant” (well done) voice is the game author's own.

Delete your account and data

“Path of Islamic Knowledge” game

You can delete your account and all its data yourself at any time, from the app or from a browser.

Steps

  1. Open the game in the app, or in a browser at engebra87.github.io/darb.
  2. Sign in to your account if you aren't signed in.
  3. In the main menu, under your name, tap “Delete account”.
  4. Tap “Delete my account permanently” to confirm.

What gets deleted

  • Your email address and password.
  • The name shown on the leaderboard.
  • Your points, level and game progress.
  • Your house and country in the “Land of the Seekers of Knowledge”.

All of this is deleted immediately and permanently. We keep no copy.

If you use the same account in another of our apps, all game data is deleted and the account stays for that app.

Can't sign in to your account?

Write to us on the feedback page with “Account deletion request” and the name you use on the leaderboard, without posting your email publicly. We'll contact you to complete the deletion within 30 days.
